Established in 2016

Crafting Heirloom Handbags & Purses For Over A Decade

Velora was founded with a singular vision: to unite timeless European leathercraft with thoughtful modern functionality. Working alongside family-owned tanneries in Tuscany, our artisans shape full-grain calfskin and sumptuous suedes into architectural totes, evening clutches, and convertible backpacks built to be cherished for generations.

Our Standards

Leather Certifications & Craftsmanship Standards

Every handbag created at Velora undergoes a 28-point atelier inspection for hide suppleness, seam tension, and clasp precision.

LWG Gold Tanneries

Sourced exclusively from Leather Working Group Gold-rated Italian tanneries with ethical traceability.

Jewelry-Grade

Solid brass clasps, turn-locks, and chains plated in 18k gold or palladium for lifelong luster.

Organic Dyes

Rich, non-toxic botanical tannins that preserve the natural breathability and aroma of full-grain leather.

Lifetime Warranty

Our Design Philosophy

Architectural Balance Meets Everyday Comfort

We believe a luxury handbag should feel featherlight on your shoulder while maintaining a sculpted silhouette. Our studio engineers weight-balanced handles, padded tech compartments, and intuitive magnetic closures.

Whether you carry a structured work tote, a crystal-clasped bridal clutch, or a convertible leather backpack, every piece is stress-tested for real-world poise.
