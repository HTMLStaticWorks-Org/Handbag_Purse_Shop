Artisan Heritage

Our 4-Step Handbag Crafting Process

Every Velora purse passes through the hands of master leather artisans in a meticulous four-stage creation ritual.

01

Leather Selection

We hand-select top-tier Italian full-grain hides for uniform grain texture, suppleness, and rich natural dye absorption.

02

Pattern & Painting

Panels are precision-cut and hand-painted with five coats of flexible edge lacquer for a seamless, fray-proof contour.

03

Saddle Stitching

Master craftspeople stitch waxed linen thread and fit custom 18k gold-plated clasps, zippers, and protective feet.

04

Monogram & Gift

Each purse is inspected, embossed with your initials if requested, wrapped in silk dust bags, and shipped in a luxury box.
